How to play

  1. Pick a host One person runs the game: opens it, reads the questions out, keeps things moving and plays referee. Think game-show presenter, not quiz master.
  2. Share one screen Put it on a TV, project it on the wall, prop a tablet in the middle of the table, or share it on Zoom or Teams. Everyone looks at the same thing.
  3. Talk, don't tap The screen asks, the people answer, out loud. Agree, disagree, ask a follow-up, tell the story behind it. That's the game.
  4. Make your own rules Keep score on paper, play in teams, vote on the best answer or don't bother with winners at all. The host decides.

About these party games

These games are a love letter to the old board games and TV game shows — the ones where the fun was never really on the board, it was the arguing, the laughing and the “wait, what would you say?” around it.

The screen is only the spark. It throws out a question, a topic or a challenge, and then the real game happens off screen, between the people in the room. Nobody needs their own device, nobody needs an account, and nobody is racing a clock.

They work at parties, in classrooms, at the pub, around the dinner table, or on a video call with the host sharing their screen.

Questions hosts ask

Do players need an account or their own device?

No. One person hosts on one screen — a TV, projector, laptop or tablet. Nobody logs in, nobody downloads anything, and there is no join code.

How is this different from Kahoot, Baamboozle or Blooket?

Those are brilliant quiz platforms where players race to tap answers. These games put nothing in players' hands: the screen asks, and people answer out loud to each other. They are built for talking, not tapping.

Are they good for ESL and speaking classes?

Yes. They were made for speaking practice — warmers, fluency work and conversation classes — and work for teens and adults at intermediate level and up.

Can I play on Zoom or Teams?

Yes. The host shares their screen and runs the game; everyone else just talks.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no premium tier, no sign-up. Just play.
