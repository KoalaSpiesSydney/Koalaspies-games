I Beg to Differ

A parlour game of polite disagreement

The instruction

Whatever statement you are given, no matter what it says, you MUST politely disagree.

Your opposition picks a plaque. You speak against it for up to 60 seconds, even if you secretly agree. It builds critical thinking, tact and empathy.

So break out of your bubble and experience somebody else's bubble.

The dinner table rule

In the old days Grandma said you should never speak about sex, religion or politics at the dinner table. The dinner table rule applies here.

How you score
  • 10for every 10 seconds you speak, up to 60 seconds (when the timer is on)
  • 1for disagreeing
  • 5for doing it politely
  • 15for giving evidence or reasons
  • 20for explaining
  • −5up to 5 off for repeating yourself (your opposition chooses 0 to 5)
  • −5up to 5 off for going on a tangent and speaking off topic (0 to 5)

Your opposition fills out your scorecard. They must be fair and consistent, because their own turn to be scored is coming. A turn never scores below zero.

Nine cards are on the table. When all nine have been played the game is over, and the highest score wins.
