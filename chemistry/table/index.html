Seven Ways to Revise the Periodic Table

Six matching games, easiest first. Each one asks a different question about the same chemistry: what a symbol stands for, what an element is used for, where it is dug out of the ground, what an everyday substance is really called, what is inside a formula, and what a reaction makes. Play them in order or drop into the one that matches the topic you are teaching this week.

76Games
118Elements
232Question cards

Teachers Project a game on the board and play it as a class, or hand the link to students for revision. Every game deals six fresh pairs each round and keeps going until the whole list has been covered, so it never runs out mid-lesson.

Students Work through them on your own, on a phone, tablet or computer. Nothing is timed and nothing is scored — turn over a card, read the clue, and see whether you can place it.
