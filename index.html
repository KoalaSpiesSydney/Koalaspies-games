About Koalaspies games

Koalaspies makes warmers, old-school revision, social games and speaking practice for classrooms, parties and families, for kids and kids at heart. Every game runs in the browser on one shared screen, so a whole room can play together.

Are the games really free?

Free, always. There is no subscription and nothing asks you to sign up. The site is supported by advertising.

Do players need an account?

No. Open a game and start playing. There is nothing to install and no login.

How do I play with a class or group?

Project a game on the board or wall, or put a tablet in the middle of the table. Most games are built for teams taking turns on one screen.

Can I play on my own?

Yes. Almost every game works alone on a phone. Scan the QR code at the top of the page to open it on another device or share it.

Can I get a game made for my course?

Yes. A commissioned game uses your content, branding and colours, and comes as a single HTML file you own, ready for Moodle, Canvas, a website or a USB stick. See below.
