Games Made to Play Together

Warmers, old-school revision, social games and speaking practice — for kids and kids at heart.

Free, always. Nothing is timed, nothing is scored, and nothing asks you to sign up.

Classes and groups: project a game on the board or wall, or put a tablet in the middle of the table.

Students and individuals: almost every game works alone on a phone. Scan the QR code in the corner to share.
