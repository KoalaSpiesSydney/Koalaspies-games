Free tools for teachers

Certificates and reports

Free, with no sign-up. Both tools work entirely in your browser, so nothing you type is sent to us or stored online. What makes each certificate and report real is what you add: your logo and your signature.

  • Certificates🏅

    Bespoke Certificate Maker

    Free certificates for one student or a whole class. Nothing you enter is uploaded or kept by us, and students' names are gone as soon as you close the browser. You add your school's logo and sign each certificate by hand, and that is what makes it unique and meaningful to your students.

    Make a certificate
  • Reports📝

    Bespoke Report Maker

    Free Word reports with your own headings and sections. Nothing you enter is uploaded or kept by us, and your class list is gone as soon as you close the browser. Your logo and your signature are what give each report its credibility.

    Make your reports

Printable worksheets in minutes

Professional-looking worksheets, without the formatting. Type or paste your own words, click to choose what to practise, and download a clean PDF with an answer key (or a caller's list for bingo), ready to print or project. Free, no sign-up and no ads on your worksheets. Everything stays in your browser: nothing you type is sent to us or stored online.

  • Bingo🎲

    Bespoke Bingo Card Maker

    Free printable bingo cards from your own words, or from ready-made vocabulary lists. Every student gets a different card, in 3 × 3, 4 × 4 or 5 × 5, with a caller's list for you. Nothing you type is uploaded or kept by us.

    Make bingo cards
  • Worksheets🧾

    Bespoke Gap-Fill Maker

    Free gap-fill (cloze) worksheets from any text. Paste it, click the words to blank out, and print a worksheet with a word box and an answer key. Nothing you paste is uploaded or kept by us.

    Make a worksheet
  • Word order🔀

    Bespoke Sentence Scramble Maker

    Free jumbled-sentence worksheets from your own sentences. Type them in, choose slashes or boxes, and print a word-order worksheet with writing lines and an answer key. Nothing you type is uploaded or kept by us.

    Make a worksheet
  • Listening🎧

    Bespoke Song Gap-Fill Maker

    Turn any song into a listening worksheet. Paste the lyrics, click the words to blank out or gap the rhymes in one click, and print a worksheet with a QR code to the song and an answer key. The lyrics never leave your browser and are gone when you close the page.

    Make a song worksheet

Interactive video lessons

For students on their own devices. Turn a YouTube video into a lesson, then share a link or QR code. Each student answers at their own pace and sees their score at the end.

  • Video lessons🎬

    Bespoke Video Lesson Maker

    Add questions that pause any YouTube video: multiple choice, gap-fill, true or false, matching, put in order and short answer. Share a link or QR code with your class. No sign-up, and nothing is stored online.

    Make a video lesson

About Modern English Australia

  • Games FROM your phone, NOT on your phone — to share and use like a game show or board game.
  • Practice tests for IELTS, PTE & CAE.
  • Mini-courses in grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation that are often skipped over by traditional textbooks.
  • Music-based lessons and ESL songs to make mastering English faster, smarter and fun!
  • Free teacher tools: interactive video lessons, and certificate, report, bingo card, gap-fill, sentence scramble and song gap-fill makers.

Designed as low-prep, highly engaging, high-energy digital EFL lessons for teachers, they also work just as well for self-study. Project them as part of a lesson, share them with a class, or study on your phone to prepare for the games and activities in upcoming classes. Most lessons have a QR code in the top left corner to share them.
