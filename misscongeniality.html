How to play

Miss Congeniality
  1. Get four in a row to win. Across, down or diagonally — the first team to claim four squares in a line takes the game.
  2. Win a square by answering like a finalist. Listen to the question, then speak for the whole time on the clock — smile, take a breath, and keep going. Stop early and the square stays open.
  3. Three hints each. A team may press Hint to read the question on the screen — but only three times in the whole game, so save them for the hard ones.
