Why sport is important

A Nelson Mandela speech — watch it, answer three questions, then say what you think.

▶ Watch the speech Step 1

Watch it in full. Mandela explains the power sport has to bring people together and change what they believe is possible. Take notes — the questions below ask about the ideas in the speech, not the facts around it.

✎ Three questions Step 2

Choose the best answer for each one, then press Check answers.

One
According to Mandela, what does sport create “where once there was only despair”?
Two
Mandela repeats the phrase “It has the power to…” three times in a row. Why does he do this?
Three
Mandela says sport “speaks to youth in a language they understand.” What does he most likely mean by this?
🗣 Now say it out loud Step 3

Do you agree or disagree with Mandela that sport has the power to change the world?

Tell a friend, a family member or your teacher what you think — and give two reasons for it. Keep going for thirty seconds if you can.
