What matters most

A talking game for one to four players.

  1. Pick a balloon. It turns over and shows a topic — anything from dogs to the rule of law.
  2. Talk about it. Press Start talking and keep going.
  3. Watch the clock. It counts to thirty seconds and stops itself.
  4. The room decides. The clock counts the seconds; the players judge whether the talking counted. Nudge the points up or down before awarding.
  5. The balloon is spent. It leaves the board. When the last one goes, the game is over.

Harder topics pay more. It is the mark you reach, not the marks added up — 25 seconds is 2 points.

Playing fair

  • Take it in turns — the scoreboard shows whose go it is.
  • A long silence ends the turn; the others say when.
  • Counting, listing the alphabet or repeating yourself is not talking.
  • Stuck? Press Skip — the balloon still goes, but nobody scores.
