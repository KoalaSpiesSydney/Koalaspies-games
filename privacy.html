1. Who we are

Koalaspies is a free games website made by Justine Kelly in Sydney, Australia. In this policy, "we" and "us" means Koalaspies and Justine Kelly.

2. What we collect

  • No accounts. You don't sign up, log in or give us your name to play.
  • Game progress stays on your device. Some games may remember your settings or progress in your own browser (called "local storage"). That information stays on your device; we don't receive it. Clearing your browser data deletes it. [CHECK]
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, and we don't sell or share it.
  • Basic server logs. Like almost every website, our web host may automatically record technical details such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running. [CHECK — name your host, e.g. GitHub Pages]

3. Advertising and cookies

Koalaspies is supported by advertising so that the games can stay free. Ads are clearly labelled "Advertisement". Anything labelled "Koalaspies Shop" is our own store, not a paid ad.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some other vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.
  • You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The games will still work.

4. Other services this site uses

  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.
  • QR codes (in the header and the share section) are generated by goqr.me (api.qrserver.com). To make the code, the address of the page you're on is sent to that service.
  • Images in the shop panel load from Adobe Portfolio (myportfolio.com).
  • The Koalaspies Shop is run on a separate platform with its own privacy policy. Nothing you do there is shared back to this site.

5. Children, schools and teachers

Many Koalaspies games are suitable for children and students, and they are designed to be run by an adult — a teacher, parent, carer or group host — on a shared screen. We don't ask players for any personal information, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

On pages intended for children, we ask our advertising partners to show only non-personalised ads, and we don't allow ads to be targeted to children based on their behaviour. [CHECK — this is true only once you tag those pages as child-directed in AdSense]

Teachers using the games in class: no student data is entered or stored by us, so no student accounts or parental consent forms are needed on our side. Please follow your school's own policy on websites that carry advertising.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we'll delete it.

6. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you (usually none, or just an email), ask us to correct or delete it, or complain about how we've handled it. We aim to reply within 30 days. Australian visitors can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. Visitors in the EU/UK have rights under the GDPR, and California residents under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to opt out of personalised advertising. We do not sell personal information.
