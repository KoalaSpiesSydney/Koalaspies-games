Element Symbols – Periodic Table Matching Game

About this game

A free periodic table game for learning the chemical symbols. Players choose a symbol, such as Na, Fe or W, then turn over cards to find the element it stands for: sodium, iron or tungsten.

It covers all 118 elements, six at a time, so a class can play a round or two or work through the whole table. Each round takes a few minutes. Play it on one shared screen as a class starter, or let students scan the QR code and play on their phones.
